[~1:15] [From Version 1 — about 20 seconds] And these challenges matter. Under the Philippine CPD Act, RA 10912, teachers are required to keep upgrading their skills — and choosing the right training needs objective evidence of their gaps. Evaluation also has real stakes: in an OECD study on teacher appraisal, 11 of 19 countries link evaluation results to salary. So evaluation must be structured, consistent and transparent.
