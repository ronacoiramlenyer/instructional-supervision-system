[~7:35] 6. RESULTS AND FINDINGS Now, let us look at the findings of the study. To complement the system-generated data and documented refinements, a system evaluation survey was administered to ten Subject Coordinators using a five-point Likert scale. The results were particularly encouraging. Monitoring Teacher Performance received a mean of 4.81. Data Accessibility and Interpretability received 4.85. Decision Support for Coaching and Professional Development received 4.83. Evidence-Based Contribution to Merit Rating received 4.89. And System Improvement and Responsiveness also received 4.89. All five constructs obtained mean ratings ranging from 4.81 to 4.89 out of 5. The results indicate a consistently positive evaluation among the respondents.
