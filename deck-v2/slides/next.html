[Backup slide — show only if someone asks about next steps] Thank you for that question. At present, the Instructional Supervision System covers two stages of the instructional cycle: planning, through Curriculum Map and Learning Guide Analytics, and implementation, through Classroom Observation Tool Analytics. The natural next step is evaluation — determining whether students actually achieved the intended learning outcomes. This is the focus of the Learning Outcomes Analytics, which is now being developed. With it, the system completes the cycle of planning, implementation, and evaluation, consistent with Constructive Alignment. It also responds to one of the limitations of this study, which does not yet cover teacher-made assessments or student learning outcomes. The results of the Learning Outcomes Analytics will then feed back into planning, so that the cycle of improvement continues. [Reminder: the live app requires an LSGH account and may show actual data. For an external audience, describe it instead of opening it.]
