[~6:50] The outputs of the three analytics subsystems are also integrated into the institutional merit rating system. Curriculum Map Analytics contributes to Domain 1: Curriculum Planning and Development. Learning Guide Analytics contributes to Domain 2: Content Knowledge and Pedagogy. And Classroom Observation Tool Analytics contributes to Domain 3: Instruction. The results are consolidated across three trimester evaluations and used as one of the bases for institutional merit rating. This establishes a more structured and evidence-based approach to evaluating teacher performance.
