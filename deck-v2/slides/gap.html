02 · The problem

The problem was not the absence of data. It was how we managed, connected, and used the data.

Performance trends were hard to see

Training needs were hard to pinpoint

Merit rating was slow to consolidate

[~2:10] As a result, identifying performance trends, determining specific training needs, and consolidating information for merit rating became difficult and time-consuming. The problem was not the absence of data. The problem was how we managed, connected, and utilized the evaluation data. This led me to develop an integrated system that would make instructional supervision more systematic, measurable, and data-driven.
