Sources: Republic Act 10912 (2016); OECD (n.d.); Darling-Hammond et al. (2017)

(~40 sec) Two reasons this matters beyond our school. First, RA 10912 — the CPD Act of 2016 — requires every professional, teachers included, to keep updating and upgrading. You can only choose the right training if you know your gaps objectively. Second, evaluation has stakes. In an OECD study on teacher appraisal, 11 of 19 countries tie evaluation results to salary. Here at LSGH, merit rating feeds ranking and promotion. Darling-Hammond reminds us good PD is sustained and informed by feedback and reflection. So if evaluation affects growth and pay, it has to be structured, consistent and transparent.
