In coordinators’ words

“…ang kalakasan at kailangan pang paunlarin ng mga guro at ng department.”

“…design more targeted and specific action plans.”

“Averages of the three components (CM, LG, and COT)… were available.”

“…to see specific areas that need improvement” — in vertical meetings.

(~40 sec) What coordinators wrote in the open-ended items. Read the big one in Filipino, then translate: “the strengths, and what still needs developing, of the teachers and of the department.” The system shows both — for each teacher and for the whole department. Then the three smaller quotes: - “Design more targeted and specific action plans to address these areas of concern” — training needs, answered. - The CM, LG and COT averages ready for performance appraisal — merit rating, answered. - Specific areas for improvement in CM and LG, used in vertical meetings — conversations are now about indicators, not impressions. Teachers also became more reflective, because they receive indicator-based feedback automatically.
