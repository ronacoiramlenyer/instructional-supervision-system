Instructional Supervision System · Reynel Mario Canor · La Salle Green Hills · DLSU Conference 2026

(~45 sec) This prompt is for the AUDIENCE, not for an AI. The goal is to put everyone in the coordinator’s seat, so they follow the rest of the talk from that point of view. Say: “We all know how to prompt an AI — ‘Act as a…’. Today, I’m prompting you.” Read the prompt slowly, looking at the audience: “Act as a subject coordinator. Review every teacher’s curriculum map, learning guide and classroom observation for three trimesters. Rate each one fairly for merit. Tell each exactly what to improve. Find your department’s training needs. And — show your evidence.” Point to the green line: “For the next eleven minutes, you are the coordinator. Keep that hat on — every part of this talk answers one line of that prompt.” Optional, if the room is warm: “Quick show of hands — who has ever rated a colleague, or been rated, with only impressions to go on?”
