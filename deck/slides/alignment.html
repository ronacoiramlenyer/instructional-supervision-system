(~60 sec) RQ2 — the design. The theory is Biggs’ Constructive Alignment: learning works when outcomes, teaching activities and assessment line up. So instead of judging a teacher on one observation, the system follows the whole chain: - PLAN — CM Analytics, 28 indicators across Real-World Inquiry, Concept-building, Integration and Transfer, and Self-evaluation. - DESIGN — LG Analytics, 34 indicators across the nine instructional designs, ID1 to ID9. - IMPLEMENT — COT Analytics, 20 look-fors: 13 on the teacher, 7 on the students. That’s 82 indicators in total, all drawn from the LSGH Curriculum Framework and approved by the school before use. Planning, design and implementation — assessed together, not in isolation.
