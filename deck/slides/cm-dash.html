(~50 sec) RQ3 — how the system displays the data. Point to the screen: this is the actual CM Writing Performance dashboard, third trimester, SY 2025–2026. The large radar is the whole institution; the small ones are per subject area. How to read it: the fuller the radar, the stronger the performance. Every dip is an indicator where teachers are falling short — that’s a training need, made visible. Example from the LG dashboard: dips clustered on ID5 — deep processing and higher-order thinking. That tells us exactly where to focus professional development, while the strong areas become benchmarks.
