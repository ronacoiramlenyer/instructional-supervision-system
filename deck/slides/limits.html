Limitations and recommendations

Honest limits, clear next steps

Limitations

  • No causal claim on teacher or student gains
  • Teacher-made assessments not analyzed
  • Indicators specific to LSGH
  • Only as good as the evaluator’s input
  • Google Sheets limits scale and security

Recommendations

  • Keep calibrating the scoring model
  • Tighten access control and user roles
  • Standardize teacher identity records
  • Add a report preview before sending
  • Review indicators periodically
(~45 sec) Being honest about the limits: - This is a design-and-development study, so I can’t claim the system caused teacher improvement or learning gains. - It covers CM, LG and classroom implementation — not teacher-made assessments. - The indicators are LSGH-specific; another school would need to adapt them. - Output depends on the quality of what coordinators encode. - Google Sheets and Apps Script have limits on scale and security. Next steps: keep calibrating the scoring model; secure access so hidden sheets aren’t exposed; a standard teacher ID to avoid mismatched names; a preview before reports are sent; and periodic review of indicators against the Curriculum Framework and our Mission-Vision.
