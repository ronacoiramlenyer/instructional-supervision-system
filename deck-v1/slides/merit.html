(~50 sec) This is where RQ3 pays off — merit rating. Each subsystem maps to a domain of the school’s merit rating form: - CM → Domain 1, Curriculum Planning and Development — 5% - LG → Domain 2, Content Knowledge and Pedagogy — 5% - COT → Domain 3, Instruction — 15% The system averages the teacher’s scores across the three trimesters, and that average lands in a band: 3.51 to 4.00 is “Exceeds expectations,” and so on. So a full quarter of the Professional Commitment component now rests on documented, indicator-level evidence — not on impressions. That’s the “show your evidence” line from the prompt.
