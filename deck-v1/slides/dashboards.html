(~55 sec) RQ3 — how the system displays the data. These are the real dashboards, third trimester, SY 2025–2026. Left — CM and LG: radar charts show every indicator at once, for the whole school and for each subject. A full radar is strong performance; a dip is an indicator where teachers fall short — a training need, made visible. In the LG data, dips cluster on ID5, higher-order thinking, so that’s where PD goes. Right — COT: observation happens in real time, so gauges compare unit and department means, and bars show each look-for. The completion tracker shows who still needs to be observed — 99.55% in this snapshot. Teachers, meanwhile, get their own PDF report right after evaluation.
